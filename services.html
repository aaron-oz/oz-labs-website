Spatio-Temporal Modeling

Our deepest expertise. Over a decade building spatial and spatio-temporal models for global health, environmental science, and genomics. We've produced 5×5km resolution maps of health outcomes across Africa and low- and middle-income countries, published in Nature, Nature Medicine, The Lancet, and the New England Journal of Medicine.

Whether you need to model disease prevalence across a continent, predict environmental change over time, or analyze spatially-resolved gene expression data — we bring methods grounded in decades of statistical theory to problems with real spatial and temporal structure.

  • Gaussian process models and kriging (geostatistical and lattice data)
  • Spatio-temporal dynamics and forecasting
  • Point process models (Cox processes, log-Gaussian Cox processes)
  • SPDE/INLA approximations for scalable spatial inference
  • Spatial transcriptomics and computational biology applications
  • High-resolution geospatial mapping and uncertainty quantification
  • Custom spatial data visualization and interactive mapping tools
